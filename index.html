GLO Marketplace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

GLO Marketplace ("GLO", "we", "our") is a hyper-local business directory application for Ethiopian cities, connecting buyers and sellers directly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number and name — required to create an account and to allow buyers to contact sellers.
  • City and business category — to show relevant local listings.
  • Location (GPS) — only when you tap "Use My Location", to sort nearby shops by distance. You can deny this permission and the app still works with a default area.
  • Photos — only those you choose to upload for your profile, product posts, or seller verification documents.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Chapa (chapa.co). We never store your card, TeleBirr, or CBE Birr credentials. We store only the transaction reference and status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account (via SMS one-time code).
  • To display your listings to other users of the app.
  • To sort listings by distance when you enable location.
  • To process subscription and boost payments through Chapa.
  • To verify seller identity when you voluntarily submit documents.
  • To prevent fraud, spam, and abuse.

3. What We Share

Chapa (https://chapa.co/) Seamless Payments , Endless Opportunities! Empowering businesses in Ethiopia to securely accept online payments from international and local customers by providing fast and easy payment experience.
  • With other users: your business name, city, category, posts, profile photo, and phone number (so buyers can contact you). This is the core purpose of a marketplace directory.
  • With service providers: Google Firebase (data storage and authentication), Cloudinary (image hosting), Africa's Talking (SMS delivery), Chapa (payment processing), and MapTiler (map tiles). Each processes data only to provide their service.
  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers protected by industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and security rules that restrict access. Verification documents are visible only to GLO administrators.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Product posts are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • You may delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and associated data.
  • For deletion requests or questions, contact us at the email below.

6. Children

GLO is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced inside the app. Continued use after changes means acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: glomarketplace.et@gmail.com
