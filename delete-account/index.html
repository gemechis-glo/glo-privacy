Delete Your GLO Market Account

GLO Market — Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your GLO Market account and the data associated with it. GLO Market is operated by GLO Market and Trading One Member PLC.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account, send an email to gloappsupport@gmail.com from the phone number or email address registered to your account, or include your registered phone number in the message so we can verify the request.

Please use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and state that you want your GLO Market account deleted.

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and process the deletion. You will receive a confirmation once it is complete.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your profile information (name, phone number, city, and shop details)
  • All of your listings and their photos
  • Your saved items, follows, and in-app messages
  • Your verification documents, if you submitted any

This data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

What We Keep, and Why

We may retain a limited set of records after your account is deleted:

  • Payment and transaction records — kept where required for legal, tax, and accounting obligations, in line with applicable Ethiopian law. These records are retained only as long as legally required and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Records we are legally required to preserve in connection with a dispute, investigation, or legal request.

All other personal data is deleted as described above.

For general privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy or contact gloappsupport@gmail.com.
